# 鴨 Dakku demo

A playable demo of [Dakku](https://hangalex.github.io/dakku/), my study timer where every focus session hatches a
little duck into your pond. It's a snapshot of my demo pond full of made-up ducks, and it runs entirely in your
browser: start a timer and hatch a duck, join Mina while she studies, try the holiday events and ponds. Nothing you do
leaves your phone, and it starts fresh each day.

**Open it:** https://hangalex.github.io/dakku/ on a phone or a computer.

**On a phone, for the best experience:** open the link in **Safari** (iPhone) or **Chrome** (Android), not inside another app like Instagram or Discord, then add it to your Home Screen: on iPhone tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**, on Android tap ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**. It then opens full-screen from its own icon, like an app.
