Focus, and a duck hatches. On every device.

To get in, open the invite link you were sent, or type the 6-digit code from Dakku on your other device (Settings → Add a device).

Tip: add Dakku to your Home Screen first: tap then Add to Home Screen. Open it from there and sign in once.

Where do I find the code?

On the PC that runs Dakku, open http://localhost:8640, then 設定 Settings → Add a device for a 6-digit pairing code. The five-word passcode is under Show passcode on the same page.
