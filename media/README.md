# Media

The screenshots and animations on the front page. They're all from the demo, so every duck, name and number in them is
made up. The stills are see-through outside the phones and windows, and the animations are WebPs of whole frames.

- `hero.webp`: the banner, three phones (Christmas, the pond, Together)
- `hatch.webp`, `golden.webp`: a session's last seconds and the hatching, on Halloween and with the Golden duck
- `pond-phone.png`, `pond.webp`, `ponds.webp`, `ponds.png`, `decorations.png`: the ponds
- `ducks.png`: every duck, shelf by shelf, from `art/ducks`
- `gallery.png`, `duck-page.png`, `gallery-az.png`, `picker.png`, `golden-page.png`: the gallery and Choose a duck
- `event-*.webp`: the four holiday events, the ring around the timer and then the pond
- `themes.png`: the four themes
- `join.webp`, `send-cheer.webp`, `cheer.webp`, `together.png`, `cheers.png`: focusing together
- `stats.png`, `year.png`, `year-card.png`: stats and the shareable year picture
- `desk-pond.png`, `desk-together.png`: on a computer
- `demo-qr.png`: the demo's address as a code
