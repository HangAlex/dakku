# The app

What you see on your phone or computer: one page, installable as an app (a progressive web app).

- `index.html`, `app.css`: the page and its look, with the themes
- `app.js`: everything on screen, and syncing with the server (it keeps working offline)
- `art.js`: drawing the ducks, eggs and ponds, and where everything floats
- `kinds.js`: the list of ducks (written by `scripts/duck.py`)
- `lofi.js`, `lofi.json`: the lofi player and its playlist
- `qr.js`: QR codes, for invites and adding a device
- `sw.js`: the service worker: offline use and notifications
- `ducks/`: every picture: the ducks, eggs, ponds, ripples and decorations
- `icons/`: the app icon
